## Hosting and operating-cost hypothesis

For a **small synthetic alpha only**, a reasonable starting estimate is one DigitalOcean Basic Droplet with 4 GiB RAM, 2 vCPUs, 80 GiB SSD, and 4,000 GiB outbound transfer: **US$24/month**. The bundled plan includes a public IPv4 address; inbound transfer is free. These are list prices before applicable tax; the founder still needs to select a region based on buyer geography, data-location obligations, and support needs. This is a sizing hypothesis, not measured capacity. Build container images off-host. The estimate assumes app and PostgreSQL named volumes use the Droplet’s included persistent disk, with no extra block volume; B must confirm fit and recovery suitability.

| Cost item                      |     Planning amount | Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Basic Droplet                  |        $24.00/month | 4 GiB / 2 vCPU / 80 GiB / 4,000 GiB transfer.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Weekly Droplet backups         |         $4.80/month | DigitalOcean lists weekly backups at 20% of Droplet cost; four weekly points are retained. **Subtotal: $28.80/month before tax.**                                                                           |
| Optional Spaces object storage |    $5.00/month base | 250 GiB standard storage. With this option, the simple subtotal is $33.80/month before tax. This is a separate DigitalOcean service, but it is not an independent account/provider recovery plan by itself. |
| Optional block volume          | $0.10 per GiB/month | DigitalOcean states attached volumes are not included in Droplet backups; volume snapshots are $0.06/GiB/month. Avoid adding one until its backup and restore path is included in B’s runbook.              |

The official Hetzner cost-optimized page currently marks the CX23 plan unavailable, so its lower historical price is not a purchase recommendation. Hetzner’s June 2026 adjustment lists a European CX23 price of $6.49/month excluding IPv4, but that listing does not override the current availability message. DigitalOcean publicly lists the Basic plan; confirm account and region availability before purchasing.

The weekly provider backup is not a substitute for a tested PostgreSQL dump/restore and a separate copy of the persistent early-access data. Select an encrypted off-host destination, retention window, recovery point/objectives, and named restore owner. The $5 Spaces line is only a budget placeholder; the founder should decide whether a different account or provider is required for recovery from account compromise. No off-host restore is claimed as complete.

Operational assumptions to confirm in the [staging activation and operations guide](../deploy/staging/README.md): a single small VM, public web traffic through the TLS proxy, administration restricted to founder-approved IPs, and no public PostgreSQL/Auth/admin ports. There is no measured load capacity or hosted alert destination configured; the runbook documents local probes and incident steps. Name an alert recipient and owner for uptime, TLS renewal, disk/memory pressure, database readiness, early-access storage, and backup freshness before any hosted access.

## Product and discovery hypothesis

**Positioning to test:** Hirearchy helps engineering reviewers inspect saved work and task activity, then write a human-owned review. It does not score a candidate or recommend a hiring outcome.

**Initial customer hypothesis:** Small and mid-sized software teams that hire backend or platform engineers repeatedly, with an engineering leader who owns assessment quality and a small group of senior reviewers. The problem to validate is whether reviewers need more shared context to reconstruct and discuss submitted work. This is not validated by interviews; there are no customer results, active pilots, testimonials, or proven willingness-to-pay.

**Pilot price hypothesis:** One supervised 30-day team pilot, one approved scenario, up to 10 sessions, and onboarding for one recruiter plus two evaluators, at **US$250 total**. This is a question for discovery, not an offer or validated price. Do not quote or charge it until hosting, privacy/legal approval, support ownership, candidate assistance, and actual costs are accepted.
