### Identity boundary discovered

The disposable `r23.create_session` function is executable by `r23_gateway` and accepts an asserted subject ID, Auth session ID, assurance, and expiry without verifying an Auth credential inside PostgreSQL. A direct gateway SQL caller can therefore mint a session for a chosen subject; a later lookup using a caller-settable setting does not repair that boundary. The HTTP login verifies tokens against Auth before calling the adapter, but that does not independently protect the SQL entry point. The disposable R212/R213 evaluator functions also accept reviewer IDs directly, and migrations 0009/0010 include direct grants based on caller-settable identity context. These must not be treated as persistent staging APIs or positive proof of SQL-level tenant isolation.

Lockdown additionally disables the R23/R26/R27 disposable lanes at their server-side configuration boundary even when their development flags are set. The real Auth-verification and least-privilege contracts must be established before introducing a usable internal product profile.
